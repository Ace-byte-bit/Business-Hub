About Business Hub Rwanda

Business Hub Rwanda is a remote-work marketplace built for rural Rwandans — funded by commercial advertising and anchored in verified digital identity.

Why we exist

Unemployment sits at 13.4%, with youth hardest hit. Over 72% of Rwandans live in rural areas where local jobs are scarce — but remote work can cross that distance. The problem? Scams, ghost listings, and distrust keep rural workers out.

Business Hub Rwanda fixes this with two things:

  • Verified digital identity — workers prove their national ID, employers get trusted applicants.
  • Commercial advertising — employers pay to feature jobs. That revenue keeps the platform free for workers.

How trust works

When you verify, we check your 16-digit national ID number and full name against the national registry. If they match, you get a badge that shows up everywhere on Business Hub Rwanda.

Employers who advertise are also checked — so you never apply to a company that doesn't exist. Every job listing shows the ✓ Verified employer badge only after we confirm the business.

How we stay free for workers

Employers pay for advertising plans (starting at 15,000 RWF). That revenue covers the platform. Workers never pay to browse, apply, or get verified.

What we don't do

  • ❌ No data selling — your ID number never leaves the verification check.
  • ❌ No unverified job listings — every employer is checked.
  • ❌ No pay-to-apply — workers stay free.

Built in Rwanda

No frameworks, no template builders. Hand-coded HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Designed to work on low-end phones and slow connections.
